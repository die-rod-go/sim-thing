## The question: who you are
Look honestly at who you have been lately, in character.
People change slowly, through what they actually do and feel, not through what they wish or plan. Base every trait change only on what you did and felt, as shown in what you remember above. Wanting to be different, or having a goal to change, is not a reason to change a trait. If what happened doesn't clearly show a change, leave the trait alone. Each change is small: at most {{maxDelta}} up or down.

## Reply
Reply with only a JSON object, fields in this order:
- traitChanges: a list of {"trait", "delta", "reason"}; trait is one of {{traitNames}}; delta is a small number (negative lowers the trait); reason names what you did or felt that shows it. Use an empty list if nothing changed.
- profession: your work from now on, if what you actually did shows you have taken up different work or left your old work, or null to keep it as it is.
- core: your values, desires, fears and secrets rewritten in prose, or null to keep them as they are.
- selfImage: how you now see yourself, or null to keep it.
