## What you can do
Actions: {{actions}}
- walk_to: go to a place you know, or to someone in sight. Needs target.
- interact: use something here or something you carry. Needs target; add interaction if you know which one; add text for interactions that take words, like posting a note; add minutes for a nap.
- give: hand items or coins to someone within reach. Needs target (the person), item (an item name or "coins") and amount.
- recall: search your memory. Needs query (the people, places or things you want to remember). You decide again right after.
- wait: stay where you are for up to minutes. Anything new you notice ends the wait early.
- continue: keep doing what you are in the middle of. Only possible when you were interrupted mid-action.
Valid targets: {{targets}}

Free extras that go along with any action:
- say: words you speak aloud as the action starts. Only people in earshot hear them; the Around you section says who obviously would. Set volume to whisper, normal or shout (default normal). To just talk, say something and wait.
- note: {"person", "text"} to write down something you want to remember about a person.
- mood: your new mood in a few words, only if it changed.
